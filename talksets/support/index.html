Frequently asked questions

How do I log a set by voice?

In a workout, hold the microphone button and say the exercise, the weight and the reps, for example “Bench press, 80 kilos, 10 reps”. Release the button: TalkSets shows the set and logs it. If you don’t name an exercise, the set goes to the one you’re on.

The microphone isn’t working

TalkSets needs access to the microphone and to speech recognition. On your iPhone, open Settings › TalkSets and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. Then go back to TalkSets and try again.

How does the free trial work?

The annual subscription starts with a 7-day free trial. After the trial, it renews at $49.99 per year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. You can also choose the monthly subscription at $9.99 per month, which has no free trial.

Prices are in U.S. dollars. The App Store shows the price for your country before you confirm, and charges your Apple Account.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, then tap TalkSets to change or cancel it. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you’ve already paid for.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling TalkSets, open the app and tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the subscription screen. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your workouts stay on your iPhone, with an optional backup in your own iCloud. There are no ads and no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
